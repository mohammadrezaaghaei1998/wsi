About
wsi

Wide Sky International Group of Companies,originally established as Halis Han LTD in Turkey in 2013, began its journey in the field of export and import. In 2017, the company expanded its portfolio by entering the web development sector, embracing the digital evolution of business.

In 2023, the company rebranded in Oman as Wide Sky International (WSI), marking a new era of growth and innovation. Continuing its expansion, in 2025 WSI launched Parsa Protein in Iran, focusing on meat wholesale, demonstrating the group’s diverse and dynamic business model.

Capabilities
Operating across three countries — Iran, Oman, and Turkey — WSI and its affiliated companies deliver services spanning multiple industries, combining traditional business expertise with cutting-edge technology. The company’s export and import activities cover Russia, Brazil, Ukraine, Iran, and Turkey, with operations in Iran and Turkey actively facilitating trade between these markets.

In 2023, WSI reinforced its commitment to innovation by continuing operations in business development, web development, professional videography and photography, AI solutions, animation creation, marketing consultancy, marketing strategy, business development, and 3D video mapping..
